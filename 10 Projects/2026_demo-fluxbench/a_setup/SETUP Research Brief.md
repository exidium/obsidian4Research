# SETUP: Research Brief (demo)

## Objective
Understand where engineers stop trusting FluxBench Designer results and what they do instead.

## Hypotheses
1. Trust is lost at the first unexplained discrepancy.
2. Most discrepancies are configuration, not model error.
3. Sharing friction is a bigger adoption blocker than simulation accuracy.
