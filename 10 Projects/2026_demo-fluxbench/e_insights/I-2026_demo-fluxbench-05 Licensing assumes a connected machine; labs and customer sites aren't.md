# Insight: Licensing assumes a connected machine; labs and customer sites aren't

![[_raw/2026_demo-fluxbench/reels/I-2026_demo-fluxbench-05.mp4]]

## Finding
Activation and periodic check-ins need outbound network access that lab networks block and customer networks restrict.

## Severity and impact
Two participants. The designer activated from a different machine on day one; the FAE was locked out twice in front of customers and now carries a second laptop that never goes online.

## What this means
The licensing model is costing first impressions with new users and credibility in front of customers. The FAE sized the need: thirty days of grace covers any trip.

## Evidence
![[H-S0001-0001 Activation blocked on the lab network]]
![[H-S0002-0003 Second laptop as a licensing workaround]]
![[H-S0002-0004 Thirty days of offline grace]]

## Recommendations
- Add an offline grace period sized to field trips, and activation by file or code for blocked lab networks.
- Research: confirm how common blocked lab networks are across customer segments.

## Open questions
- Does the documentation portal being blocked on the same networks compound this? One participant reads app notes on a phone.
