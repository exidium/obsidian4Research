# Designs can't leave the machine they were made on, so review, support, and hand-back all run on screenshots and PDFs

## Insight
Project files refuse to open across versions, there is no link or shared representation, and no history inside a project. Every participant has built a workaround: PDF rounds for design review, rebuilding from screenshots in support, filenames with dates and initials in validation. The FAE frames the design as something that should be shared between vendor and customer like the silicon itself.

## Implications
- A forward-compatible or link-based design representation would remove the screenshot loop in support and the PDF loop in review at once.
- Design history inside the project (what changed, when) is a smaller change that addresses P-001's and P-003's versioning pains.
- Research: observe a real design review to measure how many rounds actually happen.

## Open questions
- Who else sees a design in a typical customer team, and with what tools?
- Is a read-only web view enough, or do reviewers need to edit?
