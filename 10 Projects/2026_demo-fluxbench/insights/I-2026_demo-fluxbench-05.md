# Licensing assumes a connected machine; labs and customer sites aren't

## Insight
Activation and periodic check-ins need outbound network access that lab networks block and customer networks restrict. The designer activated from a different machine; the FAE carries a second laptop that never goes online. A thirty-day grace period would cover the FAE's trips.

## Implications
- An offline grace period sized to field trips is the direct ask; the lab-network case suggests activation should also work by file or code.
- Research: confirm how common blocked lab networks are across customer segments.
