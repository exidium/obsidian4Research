## Summary
- Hardware design engineer building a 48 V to 12 V, 6 A buck stage for a motor controller; first-time FluxBench user, forced in by part-family models.
- Activation failed on the lab network and had to be done from a desk machine.
- Ripple result disagreed with a long-used spreadsheet by about thirty percent; cause was a switching frequency silently reset when the part was changed.
- Efficiency sweep ran eleven minutes with the UI unresponsive; BOM export needs manual swap to approved vendor parts; review happens via PDF exports.
- Thermal estimate matched the first prototype within a few degrees, which is the one result they now trust.
