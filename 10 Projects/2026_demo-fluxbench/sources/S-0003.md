## Summary
- Validation engineer at a medical device maker; receives simulated designs and proves them on hardware, documenting every discrepancy.
- Measured 89 percent efficiency against a simulated 92, and ripple 1.4 times the simulated value; kit capacitor revision explained most of the ripple gap.
- Wants loss breakdown by mechanism and CSV export of plot data; reports take a day or more, mostly retyping numbers from plots into Word.
- Temperature sweeps crash the application about one time in three with no error; crash was reported to the FAE and never heard of again.
- Trusts only what they have correlated; wants a vendor accuracy statement that quality teams would accept instead of personal correlation runs.

[00:00:11] **P-003:** I'm the person who gets the design after it's been simulated and has to prove it on hardware. So I use the tool less to design and more to compare.
